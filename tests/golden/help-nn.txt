abi nn train "<text>" | train --jsonl <path> [--field <name>] | sample --text "<corpus>" --seed <char> --n <k>

Run the miniature character-level demo trainer

This is a small local demo trainer, not a production/LLM/distributed trainer.

Subcommands:
  train        abi nn train "<text>" | train --jsonl <path> [--field <name>]
               Train the miniature local character model from inline text or a JSONL text field.
  sample       abi nn sample --text "<corpus>" --seed <char> --n <k>
               Train on <corpus>, then greedily emit k characters from the seed byte.

Arguments:
  <command>             nn subcommand (required) choices=train|sample
  --jsonl <value>       JSONL training dataset path
  --field <value>       JSONL text field (default: text)
  --text <value>       sample corpus text
  --seed <value>       sample seed character
  --n <value>       sample character count
  <input>             inline training text

Examples:
  abi nn sample --text "hello" --seed h --n 16
